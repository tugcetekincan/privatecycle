STRENGTHS

  • Warm, guided pace. Three calm steps give the privacy model room to land before any action is requested.
  • Editorial restraint. One motif, one idea per screen, and a numbered rhythm that is easy to follow.
  • Honest by construction. Local storage and the manual backup path are explained before the first tap.

TRADEOFFS

  • Three steps add friction for a returning user who already knows the product.
  • Quiet art needs precise spacing. The journal has to feel composed, not empty, at 320px.
  • Less brand theatre than B, so it leans on typography and copy to be memorable.
Best when trust and tone matter more than speed, and the first session should feel like a gentle invitation rather than a setup task.

AT A GLANCE

How the three directions differ.

All three share the same product facts and the same exits. The difference is how much room the onboarding gives the promise before it asks for a first entry — and how much illustration the team wants to own.

DIMENSION A · QUIET JOURNAL B · TIDEMARK C · OPEN DOOR
PACE Three guided steps. One idea and one decision per screen. Three visual steps. The arc sequence carries the rhythm; copy stays short. One screen. Safety context and actions share a single welcome.
VISUAL WEIGHT Quiet and literary. White space, hairlines, one journal motif. Boldest. Cropped tidal arcs and a single pink marker lead the composition. Light and app-native. A soft blush hero integrated into the first screen.
SAFETY COPY Paced: storage and backup each get a calm section. Light-medium: one compact forecast note, two ruled statements. Denser: two understated rows carry backup and estimate caveats together.
BUILD EFFORT Moderate. Three screens and two custom illustrations. Highest. Three screens and three bespoke, responsive compositions. Lowest. One new screen; Calendar and import reuse existing flows.
MAIN RISK May feel long for a returning user; spacing must stay generous at 320px. Art can overpower copy if crops are not tuned per device. Compressed nuance; the welcome has to earn trust quickly.
BEST WHEN Trust and tone lead the decision. Recommended. Brand recall matters and bespoke illustration is affordable. Speed is the priority and the change surface must stay small.
FLOW MAP

Every route ends in the existing app.

No direction adds a new data path. Skip always lands on Calendar, import keeps the user in onboarding until a file is accepted, and the last-period route uses the existing day sheet.
