Calendar decisions

The month grid is the one surface the HTML didn’t cover, so this is the piece to approve first.

1

Put the grid in a soft card Proposed

The month nav, today pill, weekday row, grid and legend live inside one 18px card. It gives the calendar the same weight as the Overview cards without changing the month-swipe animation.

2

Round the day cells Proposed

Each day is a 34px squircle inside a 41px row. Period days stay a legible run of filled cells; predicted and fertile days use soft fills; tracked non-period days get a tiny muted dot so a long history stays airy.

3

Make today a transparent ring Fix

The old marker painted a surface disc over the cell, hiding a period fill on today. The new ring is transparent, so today can be both “today” and a period day.

4

Move the legend inside the card Proposed

Six compact chips below the grid keep the meanings close to the marks. Upcoming periods and fertility stay as separate section cards with the same header rhythm as Overview and Insights.

Kept as-is: month swipe, day tap, modal flow, period/fertility colors and all data logic. This is a surface and hierarchy change, not a calendar rewrite.
